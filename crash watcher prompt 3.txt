3. Round confirmation state machine

Implement or adapt the detection logic as follows:

- WAITING: no confirmed final result.

- UNCERTAIN: OCR or visual evidence is insufficient.

Only RESULT_CONFIRMED may be forwarded to the Phase 1 strategy engine.

Require appropriate stability checks and duplicate protection.
